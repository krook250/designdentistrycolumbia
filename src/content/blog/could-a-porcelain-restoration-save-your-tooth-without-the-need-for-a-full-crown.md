## What Exactly Are Porcelain Inlays and Onlays?

The terminology might sound technical, but the concept is straightforward. When a tooth has damage that's too extensive for a simple filling but doesn't require the full coverage of a crown, porcelain inlays and onlays offer an ideal alternative. An inlay fits within the cusps (the raised points) of your tooth, similar to a filling but custom-fabricated in a dental laboratory for superior fit and durability. An onlay extends over one or more cusps, providing more comprehensive coverage while still preserving healthy tooth structure.

What distinguishes these restorations at Design Dentistry Columbia is the meticulous attention to detail that Dr. Ike brings to every case. Rather than using metal amalgam or settling for a composite filling that may not withstand long-term chewing forces, porcelain inlays and onlays are precision-crafted to match your tooth's exact specifications. The result is a restoration that's virtually indistinguishable from your natural tooth while offering exceptional strength and longevity.

The process itself reflects the high-end approach you'll experience throughout your care at Design Dentistry Columbia. Dr. Ike begins with careful preparation of the affected tooth, removing only the damaged or decayed portion while conserving as much healthy structure as possible. Detailed impressions or digital scans are then taken and sent to a skilled ceramist who crafts your custom restoration. During a subsequent visit, Dr. Ike bonds the finished inlay or onlay to your tooth using advanced adhesive techniques that create a seamless, durable result.

## Why Choose Porcelain Over Other Restoration Materials?

When you're investing in your dental health, the materials matter profoundly. Porcelain offers a combination of benefits that other options simply cannot match. Its translucent quality catches and reflects light just like natural enamel, creating restorations that blend flawlessly with your smile. Unlike metal fillings that can create dark shadows or composite materials that may discolor over time, the porcelain inlays and onlays provided at Design Dentistry Columbia maintain their beautiful appearance year after year.

Beyond aesthetics, porcelain delivers remarkable functional advantages. The material is incredibly durable, resisting the considerable forces generated during chewing while protecting the remaining tooth structure. Dr. Ike appreciates how porcelain's hardness closely resembles natural enamel, which means it won't wear down opposing teeth the way some harder materials might. This biocompatibility extends to how well your body tolerates the restoration—porcelain is non-reactive and won't trigger sensitivity issues that sometimes accompany metal restorations.
