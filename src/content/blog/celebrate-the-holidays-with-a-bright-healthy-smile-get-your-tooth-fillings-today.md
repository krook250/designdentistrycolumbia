## The Types of Fillings Available

At the office of Dr. Smith, we offer a variety of tooth fillings to help protect your teeth for a lifetime of healthy smiles. The types of fillings available include composite (tooth-colored) fillings, gold fillings, and amalgam (silver) fillings. Depending on your needs, we will be able to help you choose the best type of filling for your smile.

Composite fillings are made of a mixture of plastic and glass, and are designed to match the color of your teeth. This makes them virtually invisible to the eye and can be used to repair chipped, broken, or decayed teeth. Gold fillings are made of a combination of gold, copper, and other metals. They are strong and durable, and can last up to 15 years or more. Amalgam (silver) fillings are made of a combination of metals, including silver, tin, copper, and mercury. They are also strong and durable, and can last up to 12 years.

## The Cost and Insurance Coverage of Fillings

Dental fillings are an affordable way to restore the health of your teeth and keep your smile looking bright during the holidays. Most insurance plans cover at least a portion of the cost of fillings, so be sure to check with your insurance provider to see what your coverage is. The cost of a filling can vary depending on the type of material used, the size of the filling, and the location of the tooth, so it's important to speak to your dentist about what the best option is for your particular case.

## Tips for Maintaining a Healthy Smile During the Holidays

The holidays are a time for celebration, but it’s important to take care of your teeth and gums too. Brush your teeth twice a day with a fluoride toothpaste, floss daily, and visit your dentist regularly for check-ups and cleanings. Avoid sugary treats and drinks, as well as sticky and hard foods that can damage your teeth. If you have a toothache, get it checked out and get a tooth filling if necessary. With proper care, you can enjoy a bright, healthy smile throughout the holiday season!

## The Importance of Regular Dental Checkups

Regular dental checkups are essential to maintaining a healthy, bright smile for the holidays. With regular exams, your dentist can identify any potential issues early and provide preventive treatments to keep your teeth and gums healthy. Through regular checkups, your dentist can also provide professional teeth cleanings to remove any plaque and tartar buildup, and identify any cavities or decay that may require fillings. By taking care of your teeth now, you can ensure that your smile stays bright and healthy for the holidays and beyond.
