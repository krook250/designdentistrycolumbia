## Durability and Longevity

One of the key reasons behind the popularity of dental veneers is their exceptional durability and longevity. This enduring quality makes them a sought-after choice for individuals looking to improve the aesthetic of their smile. Unlike other dental treatments that may necessitate significant changes to the tooth structure, veneers typically involve a very conservative approach.
