**Understanding Dental Implants**

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made from biocompatible materials that provide a strong foundation for fixed or removable replacement teeth. Unlike dentures, which can slip or cause discomfort, implants offer stability and comfort, allowing individuals to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. The process involves placing a titanium post into the jawbone, which integrates with the bone over time a process known as osseointegration creating a permanent base for crowns or bridges.
