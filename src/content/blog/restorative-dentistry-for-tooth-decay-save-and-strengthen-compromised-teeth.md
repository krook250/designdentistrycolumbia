**Restorative dentistry for tooth decay**

Understanding the causes of tooth decay is essential in the context of restorative dentistry for tooth decay. Tooth decay occurs when bacteria in the mouth produce acids that erode the enamel, leading to cavities. Factors such as poor oral hygiene, frequent consumption of sugary foods and drinks, and a lack of fluoride can contribute to this process. As the decay progresses, it can compromise the structural integrity of the tooth, necessitating intervention to restore and strengthen it.
