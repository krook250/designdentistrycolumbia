**The Science Behind Professional Dental Cleanings**
Understanding why professional cleanings matter begins with recognizing what happens in your mouth every single day. Plaque a soft, sticky film of bacteria constantly forms on your teeth, even within hours of brushing. When plaque isn't removed promptly, it hardens into tartar (also called calculus), a mineralized deposit that bonds to tooth enamel and cannot be eliminated with regular brushing or flossing. This is where the specialized expertise at Design Dentistry Columbia becomes essential.
Dr. Ike employs advanced scaling techniques to meticulously remove both plaque and tartar from tooth surfaces and beneath the gumline, areas particularly vulnerable to bacterial accumulation. This process isn't merely cosmetic it's preventive medicine for your mouth. Tartar buildup serves as a magnet for additional bacteria, creating an environment conducive to gum disease, tooth decay, and even systemic health complications. Research has linked oral bacteria to cardiovascular disease, diabetes complications, and respiratory infections, making your dental cleaning appointments at our Columbia, SC location a crucial component of your overall wellness strategy.
Our [preventive dentistry approach](/) ensures that every cleaning is comprehensive yet comfortable.
