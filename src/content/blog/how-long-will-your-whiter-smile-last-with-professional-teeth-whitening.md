**Lifestyle Choices That Extend Your Investment**
Your daily habits directly impact how long your professional whitening results will shine. At Design Dentistry Columbia, we guide our patients through evidence-based strategies that protect their investment in a brighter smile. The foods and beverages you consume leave their mark literally. Dark-colored liquids like coffee, tea, and red wine contain chromogens, pigmented molecules that attach to tooth enamel and cause staining over time.
Tobacco use accelerates discoloration dramatically, turning even freshly whitened teeth yellow or brown within months. Dr. Ike works with patients in Columbia, SC to develop realistic maintenance plans that accommodate their lifestyles while maximizing the longevity of their results. Perhaps you're unwilling to give up your morning coffee that's perfectly understandable. Simple adjustments like using a straw, rinsing with water afterward, or scheduling regular [professional cleanings](/) can substantially extend the brilliance of your smile.
Your oral hygiene routine also plays an essential role. Brushing twice daily with whitening toothpaste, flossing regularly, and visiting Design Dentistry Columbia for professional cleanings every six months creates the foundation for maintaining your results. These aren't just generic recommendations they're proven strategies that our patients have used successfully to keep their smiles radiant far longer than they initially expected.
