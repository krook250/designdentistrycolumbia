**Procedure Overview and Steps**

Cosmetic bonding for gaps is a straightforward dental procedure designed to close spaces between teeth with a natural-looking finish. The process begins with the dentist selecting a composite resin that matches the color of your teeth. The tooth surface is then gently etched to ensure the resin adheres properly. Next, the resin is applied and molded to fill the gap, followed by a curing process using a special light to harden the material. Finally, the bonded area is polished to blend seamlessly with the surrounding teeth, creating a flawless appearance.
