Cosmetic dentistry covers treatments that improve how your smile looks — veneers, bonding, whitening, and implants among them — while often improving function too. At Design Dentistry Columbia, Dr. Isaiah Davis builds a plan around your goals, whether that's closing a gap, brightening your smile, or replacing a damaged or missing tooth.

Your smile is one of the first things people notice, and small changes can make an outsized difference in how confident you feel showing it. At Design Dentistry Columbia, Dr. Isaiah Davis and our team offer a full range of cosmetic treatments designed around your specific goals, using state-of-the-art technology at our Columbia, SC office.

## Our Cosmetic Dentistry Services

**Porcelain veneers** are thin porcelain shells bonded to the front of your teeth, custom-shaped to address gaps, mild misalignment, or discoloration — a popular option for a comprehensive smile refresh. Learn more about our
[veneer options](/dental-veneers).

**Dental bonding** applies a tooth-colored resin material directly to teeth that are decayed, chipped, cracked, discolored, or slightly misaligned — often a faster, more conservative fix than veneers for smaller cosmetic concerns.

**Dental implants** replace missing teeth with a custom-made restoration designed to match your existing teeth, restoring both appearance and function. For patients missing several teeth, we also offer
[full-arch dental implant](/full-arch-dental-implants) solutions.

We also offer
[dental crowns](/crowns) to restore damaged or discolored teeth,
[white composite fillings](/Tooth-Fillings) for a natural-looking repair, professional teeth whitening, sealants, bridges, extractions, and broader restorative dentistry as part of a complete cosmetic plan.

## What to Expect

Every cosmetic plan starts with a consultation where Dr. Davis evaluates your teeth, discusses your goals, and recommends the combination of treatments that fits your smile, timeline, and budget. Recovery time and process vary by treatment — bonding is often completed in a single visit, while veneers or implants involve more than one appointment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is cosmetic dentistry?** Cosmetic dentistry refers to dental treatments primarily focused on improving the appearance of your teeth and smile — including veneers, bonding, whitening, and implants — though many of these treatments improve function as well.

**What are the benefits of cosmetic dentistry?** Beyond a more confident smile, many cosmetic treatments also restore damaged tooth structure, replace missing teeth, and correct bite issues that affect how you eat and speak.

**How much does cosmetic dentistry cost?** Cost varies widely depending on which treatments you choose and how many teeth are involved. We can build a personalized treatment plan and go over costs and financing options at your consultation.

**How long is recovery after cosmetic treatment?** It depends on the procedure — bonding typically has no downtime, while veneers and implants may involve a short adjustment period. We'll walk you through what to expect for your specific plan.

**How do I care for cosmetic dental work afterward?** Most cosmetic treatments are cared for like your natural teeth: brushing and flossing twice daily, regular checkups, and avoiding habits like biting hard objects that could damage the work.
