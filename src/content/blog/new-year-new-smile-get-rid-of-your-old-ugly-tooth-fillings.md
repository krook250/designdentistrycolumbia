## Benefits of Replacing Old Tooth Fillings

As we enter the New Year, it's time to take action and get rid of those old, ugly tooth fillings. Replacing your old fillings with tooth-colored fillings comes with many benefits, including improved oral health, improved aesthetics, and better overall comfort.

Not only are tooth-colored fillings more aesthetically pleasing than metal fillings, they are also safer for your mouth. Old metal fillings are prone to cavities and decay, which can lead to pain and discomfort. Tooth-colored fillings are resistant to decay and can last up to 15 years with proper care. Additionally, tooth-colored fillings are designed to bond directly to your teeth, creating a tight seal that prevents bacteria from entering and causing further damage.

## Preparation for the Procedure

Before you get your old, ugly tooth fillings replaced for a new and improved smile, it's important to do some research and preparation. First, speak to your dentist about the best materials and techniques for your particular case. Then, make sure you understand the procedure and any possible risks. Additionally, ask your dentist if there are any dietary restrictions before and after the procedure, and be sure to follow any instructions they provide.

## Aftercare for New Fillings

Taking care of your new tooth fillings is essential to ensure they last for years to come. To help maintain your new smile, brush your teeth twice a day with a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste, floss once a day, and visit your dentist regularly for checkups and cleanings. Avoid sticky and chewy foods that can pull on your fillings, and don’t bite down on hard objects like pens or ice cubes. With proper care and maintenance, your new tooth fillings will help keep your teeth healthy and strong.

## Cost Considerations for Replacing Fillings

When considering whether to replace your old, ugly tooth fillings, the cost of treatment should be taken into consideration. Generally, the cost of composite resin fillings is more than that of amalgam fillings, and the cost of replacing a filling is generally more than the cost of placing a new one. To get an accurate cost estimate for replacing your fillings, it's best to consult a dental professional.
