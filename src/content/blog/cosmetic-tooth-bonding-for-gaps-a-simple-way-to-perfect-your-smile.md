**The Bonding Procedure Explained**

Cosmetic tooth bonding is a straightforward procedure designed to enhance the appearance of your smile by addressing gaps between teeth. During the process, a dentist applies a tooth-colored resin to the affected area, which is then molded and shaped to achieve the desired look. The resin is hardened using a special light, effectively bonding it to the tooth's surface. This technique allows for a seamless integration with your natural teeth, resulting in a more uniform and aesthetically pleasing smile.
