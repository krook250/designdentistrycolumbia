## Understanding Different Types of Tooth Fillings

Tooth fillings are a common dental treatment used to repair cavities and other minor damage to the teeth. There are several types of tooth fillings available, each with its own advantages and disadvantages. Composite fillings are made from a composite resin and are designed to match the color of your natural teeth. These fillings are strong and durable, but may not last as long as other types of fillings. Amalgam fillings, also known as silver fillings, are made from a mixture of metals and are the most widely used type of filling. They are strong and long-lasting, but they may be more visible than other types of fillings. Ceramic fillings are made from porcelain and are designed to match the color of your natural teeth. They are strong and durable, but may be more expensive than other types of fillings.
