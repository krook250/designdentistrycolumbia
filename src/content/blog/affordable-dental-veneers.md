When considering affordable dental veneers, understanding the procedure and time involved is essential for those looking to enhance their smile. The process typically begins with a consultation, where a dental professional assesses the suitability of veneers for the individual's dental needs. Once deemed appropriate, the next step involves preparing the teeth, which may include reshaping the surface to ensure a perfect fit for the veneers. Once ready, the veneers are bonded to the teeth in a final appointment, resulting in a transformed smile.
