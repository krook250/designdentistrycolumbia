**What is Dental Pulp?**

The dental pulp is the innermost part of a tooth, containing nerves, blood vessels, and connective tissue. When this area becomes infected or inflamed due to decay, trauma, or other factors, it can lead to severe pain and discomfort. Recognizing the warning signs can help patients seek timely intervention from qualified professionals like Dr. Isaiah Davis.

**Common Symptoms Indicating a Possible Root Canal**

1. **Persistent Toothache:** A continuous ache that doesn't subside with over-the-counter pain relievers may signal an underlying issue with the dental pulp.
2. **Sensitivity to Hot and Cold:** If a tooth experiences heightened sensitivity to temperature changes, especially lingering sensations after exposure, it could be a sign of inflammation.
3. **Swelling and Tenderness:** Swelling around the gums near the affected tooth often indicates an infection. This tenderness may also extend to the jaw and surrounding areas.
4. **Discoloration of the Tooth:** A darkening of the tooth may suggest nerve damage or death of the pulp, warranting immediate attention.
5. **Bad Breath or Unpleasant Taste:** An ongoing bad taste in the mouth or persistent bad breath can result from an infection within the tooth.
6. **Fistula Formation:** The appearance of a small bump or pimple on the gums can indicate an abscess, which requires prompt evaluation by a dental professional.
