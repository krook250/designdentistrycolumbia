## The Technical Precision That Separates Exceptional From Average

Beyond artistic vision, the physical execution of veneer placement requires exceptional technical skill. At Design Dentistry Columbia, Dr. Ike employs advanced preparation techniques that preserve the maximum amount of your natural tooth structure while creating the ideal foundation for long-lasting, natural-looking veneers. The preparation depth, margin placement, and bonding protocols all significantly impact both the appearance and longevity of your results.

Veneers that appear bulky or overly opaque often result from inadequate tooth preparation or excessive veneer thickness. Conversely, veneers that seem translucent or fail to adequately mask underlying discoloration typically indicate insufficient material thickness or poor shade selection.
