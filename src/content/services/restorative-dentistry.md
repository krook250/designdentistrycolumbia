## Benefits of Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry is a branch of dentistry that focuses on restoring damaged or missing teeth. Restorative dentistry can help improve the appearance of your smile and improve your oral health. Dr. Isaiah Davis in Columbia, SC offers a variety of restorative dentistry services, including fillings, crowns, bridges, and implants. Fillings are used to repair cavities and restore the structure of the tooth, while crowns are used to cover and protect a damaged tooth. Bridges are used to replace missing teeth, and implants are used to replace missing teeth and provide a secure and stable foundation for replacement teeth.

Restorative dentistry can help improve the function of your teeth, as well as your overall oral health. Restorations can help protect your teeth from further damage, and can help restore your smile. Restorative dentistry can also help improve your overall confidence and self-esteem.
