## Types of Dental Crowns

At Design Dentistry Columbia, Dr. Isaiah Davis offers a variety of dental crowns to meet the needs of each patient. Dental crowns are a type of restoration used to cover a damaged tooth or to improve the appearance of a tooth.

The type of dental crown chosen depends on the condition of the patient’s tooth and the desired outcome. Porcelain and ceramic crowns are popular options for restoring front teeth as they can be matched to the color of the patient’s natural teeth. Metal crowns are a good choice for back teeth as they are strong and durable. Resin crowns are a cost-effective option for restoring teeth that are not heavily used.

## Preparation for Dental Crowns

At Design Dentistry Columbia, our experienced dentist, Dr. Isaiah Davis, is dedicated to providing the highest quality of care for our patients. Before a dental crown is placed, the tooth must be prepared to ensure a proper fit.

The preparation process begins with a thorough examination and cleaning of the tooth. X-rays may also be taken to check for any underlying issues. Our dentist will then remove any decay and shape the tooth to ensure a secure fit for the crown. In some cases, a temporary crown may be placed while the permanent crown is being made.

## Dental Crowns vs. Other Restorative Treatments

Dental crowns are a great way to restore a damaged tooth and protect it from further harm. Dental crowns are a type of dental restoration that completely covers a damaged tooth, restoring its shape, size, and strength. Crowns are made from a variety of materials, such as porcelain, metal, and resin, and are designed to match the surrounding teeth for a natural look.

When compared to other restorative treatments, such as fillings, dental crowns are a more durable and long-lasting solution. Fillings are designed to fill in the cavity and prevent further damage, but they do not provide the same level of protection as a dental crown. Dental crowns are also more aesthetically pleasing than other treatments, as they are designed to match the surrounding teeth.
