## Tooth Filling Materials

At Design Dentistry Columbia, we offer a variety of tooth filling materials to restore your teeth to their original strength and appearance. Our dental team will work with you to determine the best option for your individual needs. We offer both composite and amalgam fillings, both of which are durable and long-lasting.
