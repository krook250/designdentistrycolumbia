## Types of Dental Implants

Dental implants are a great way to replace missing teeth and restore your smile. Design Dentistry Columbia offers a variety of dental implant options to meet the needs of patients. The most common type of dental implant is the endosteal implant, which is surgically placed into the jawbone. This type of implant is a great option for patients who have enough bone structure to support the implant and who want a permanent solution to their missing teeth. Other types of dental implants include subperiosteal implants, which are placed on top of the jawbone, and mini implants, which are smaller and can be used to replace a single tooth or hold dentures in place.
