## Aftercare Instructions

At Design Dentistry Columbia, we understand that tooth extractions can be a stressful experience. We want to make sure that our patients have the best possible aftercare instructions to ensure a successful recovery.

Immediately following the extraction, it is important to apply pressure with a clean gauze pad to the extraction site. This will help to stop the bleeding. It is also important to avoid any strenuous activity or drinking through a straw for the first 24 hours.

It is important to keep the extraction site clean and free of food particles. This can be done by gently rinsing the mouth with warm salt water several times a day. It is also important to take any prescribed medications as directed by Dr. Isaiah Davis. If any pain or swelling occurs, it is important to contact Design Dentistry Columbia immediately.
