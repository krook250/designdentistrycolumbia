## Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are a popular cosmetic dentistry solution for those looking to improve the appearance of their smile. Veneers are thin shells of porcelain that are bonded to the front of teeth to provide a more aesthetically pleasing look. This procedure is a great option for individuals in Columbia, SC who want to make minor changes to their teeth, such as closing gaps, correcting misalignment, and whitening discolored teeth.
