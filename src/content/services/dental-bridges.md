## What is a Dental Bridge?

A dental bridge is a dental restoration used to replace missing teeth. It is made up of two dental crowns that are attached to the adjacent teeth and hold a false tooth in place. Dental bridges can be used to restore the natural look and function of your smile, as well as to improve your bite.

## Types of Dental Bridges

Dental bridges are a great way to replace missing teeth and restore your smile. At Design Dentistry Columbia, Dr. Isaiah Davis offers a variety of dental bridge options to suit your individual needs. Traditional bridges involve the use of two crowns to hold a false tooth in place. Cantilever bridges are used when there are teeth on only one side of the gap and are held in place by one crown. Maryland bridges are a more conservative option that uses a metal or porcelain framework to bond the false tooth to the existing teeth. Implant-supported bridges are the strongest and most secure option, as they are anchored directly to the jawbone with dental implants. No matter what type of bridge you choose, Dr. Isaiah Davis will make sure your dental bridge looks and functions like a natural tooth.

## Aftercare for Dental Bridges

Dental bridges are a great way to replace missing teeth and restore your smile. It is important to take good care of your dental bridge in order to ensure its longevity and effectiveness. Proper aftercare for dental bridges includes brushing and flossing twice a day, regular dental checkups, and avoiding certain foods that could damage the bridge.

It is important to brush your bridge gently with a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste. Flossing should be done daily to remove plaque and food particles from between the teeth and bridge. Additionally, regular dental checkups are essential to make sure the bridge is in good condition and to detect any potential problems.
