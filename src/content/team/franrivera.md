Hi, my name is Francelis, but everyone calls me Fran. I was born and raised in Puerto Rico and moved to the U.S. in 2013. It was a big change for me, but it was worth every thing I am today. I am in love with my culture; that’s why if I am not at the office juggling with chair-side assisting, lab cases, and fake laughing at Dr. Ike’s every day jokes; you can find me at home singing and dancing to my latin music or watching “telenovelas” and stand-up comedy specials.

I have been Dr. Ike’s dental assistant for 8 years and counting. At the office I pride myself on my ability of speaking 2 languages and communicating comfortably with my Spanish and English speaking patients. Building a friendly and genuine relationship with my patients is one of my favorite and most important things I get to do in Dentistry.
