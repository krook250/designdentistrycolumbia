Jerome Harper was born and raised in Columbia, South Carolina. After enlisting in the army national guard he went to midlands technical college for Dental Hygiene. He is now a talented Dental Hygienist with 3 years experience providing exceptional dental care to patients of all ages. His primary responsibility is to obtain necessary periapical, bitewing and panoramic X-rays, while providing proper cleanings and education on oral hygiene.
